How to interpret these values

absLen is summed from the family section; bin coverage is summed separately from the divergence section. These are upstream coverage counts, not a recomputed union of genomic intervals. They may differ and must not be interpreted as uniquely masked bases or an inferred unmasked fraction. The denominator is supplied by the user; record whether it includes Ns.

Source and method

No external scripts, fonts, service, login or telemetry. This helper does not modify RepeatMasker results or apply upstream class regrouping or fixed-class exclusion. The original createRepeatLandscape.pl remains available separately with its Google online runtime.
